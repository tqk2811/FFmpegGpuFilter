heart-sdf.png - signed distance field of a heart curve (x = 16 sin^2.5 t, y = 13 cos t - 5 cos 2t - 2 cos 3t - cos 4t, lower half widened
by up to 16%, height 0.914 of the width),
generated for this effect by a script (NumPy + Pillow). Grey 0.5 is the outline, darker is inside.
